## 7-Day Schedule Template

### Funnel Logic

The weekly schedule follows a funnel progression:
- **Monday-Tuesday**: Awareness (top of funnel) — reach new audiences
- **Wednesday**: Education (mid funnel) — build trust and demonstrate value
- **Thursday**: Social proof + Conversion (bottom funnel) — validate and drive purchases
- **Friday**: Engagement — deepen relationship with existing audience
- **Saturday**: Evergreen content — long-lifespan pins and posts
- **Sunday**: Nurture + Community — email digest and audience interaction

### Schedule Framework

| Day | Primary Platform | Content Type | Goal | Secondary Platform | Content Type | Goal |
|-----|-----------------|--------------|------|-------------------|--------------|------|
| Mon | TikTok | Short video (30-60s) | Awareness | Instagram Stories | Poll + BTS | Engagement |
| Tue | Instagram Reels | Short video (15-30s) | Awareness | Email | Value newsletter | Conversion |

## How to Generate a Custom Schedule

When generating a schedule for a specific product:

1. **Identify the primary goal** — If sales: weight conversion days heavier. If awareness: weight video platforms heavier.
2. **Match content to platform** — Never schedule the same content format across all platforms. Adapt:
   - TikTok: Fast-paced, trend-riding, under 60s
   - Instagram Reels: Polished, aesthetic, 15-30s
   - YouTube Shorts: Educational, value-dense, 60s
   - Pinterest: Static visual, search-optimized descriptions
   - Email: Long-form, personal, offer-driven
3. **Balance content types** — No more than 40% promotional content. Mix educational, entertaining, and community content.
4. **Respect frequency limits** — Max per platform per day:
   - TikTok: 2 posts
   - Instagram Reels: 1 post
   - Instagram Stories: 5 stories
   - Pinterest: 15 pins
   - Email: 1 send
   - YouTube Shorts: 1 post
5. **Include specific posting times** — Use the optimal times table above, adjusted to the user's timezone.

## Content Rotation Rules

To prevent audience fatigue:
- Same content type: Max 2x per week on the same platform
- Same product mention: Max 3x per week across all platforms
- Same hook style: Rotate through the 3 ad copy frameworks weekly
- Same video format: Alternate between before/after, 3-tips, and demo formats
- Promotional vs value ratio: 30% promo / 70% value across the week

## Cross-Posting Strategy

When a piece of content performs well, cross-post it with these adaptations:

| Original | Cross-Post To | Adaptation | Delay |
|----------|--------------|------------|-------|
| TikTok video | Instagram Reels | Trim to 30s, add aesthetic cover | 48 hours |
| TikTok video | YouTube Shorts | Extend title/description for SEO | 48 hours |
| Instagram Reel | Pinterest Idea Pin | Repackage as step-by-step | 72 hours |
| Email feature | Instagram Carousel | Break into swipeable tips | 1 week |
| Blog/tutorial | Email newsletter | Condense to top 3 takeaways | 2 days |
| Customer review | Instagram Story | Quote card + reaction | Same day |
