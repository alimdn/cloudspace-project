# Market Analysis Framework

## Audience Persona Template

For each product, construct a detailed persona covering these dimensions:

### Demographics
- Age range
- Geographic location
- Income level
- Education level
- Professional context (industry, job title, career stage)

### Digital Behavior
- Primary platforms (where they spend time online)
- Content consumption habits (video vs text, long-form vs short)
- Purchase triggers (impulse vs research-driven)
- Device preference (mobile-first vs desktop)

### Psychographics
- Core values and beliefs
- Aspirations and lifestyle goals
- Fears and anxieties related to the product domain
- Identity labels they resonate with

### High-Conversion Segments for Digital Products

| Segment | Description | Purchase Pattern | Best Platforms |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Creative Professionals | Designers, writers, photographers | Buy tools that enhance workflow | Instagram, Pinterest, Behance |
| Entrepreneurs/SMB | Founders, freelancers, solopreneurs | Buy time-saving and revenue-generating solutions | Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Email |
| Students/Learners | College, self-taught, career switchers | Buy affordable educational content | TikTok, YouTube, Reddit |
| Hobbyists/Enthusiasts | Gamers, crafters, fitness buffs | Buy products deepening their interests | Reddit, Pinterest, Facebook Groups |
| Tech/Developer | Engineers, data scientists, IT pros | Buy tools that automate or optimize | Twitter/X, Hacker News, Dev.to |

## Pain Points and Desires Mapping

### Method

1. List 3-5 pain points for the target audience (problems they actively want to solve)
2. List 3-5 desires (outcomes they aspire to achieve)
3. Create pairs: each pain point connects to a desire
4. Write a value proposition for each pair using: **"Stop [pain point] so you can [desire]"**

| Product Category | Top Pain Points | Top Desires |

## Audience Research Prompts

When you need to validate audience assumptions, use these research angles:

1. **Search intent analysis**: What keywords does this audience search? What questions do they ask?
2. **Community mining**: What subreddits, Facebook groups, or forums do they frequent? What do they complain about?
3. **Competitor audience**: Who follows competing products? What do they say in reviews and comments?
4. **Social listening**: What hashtags do they use? What content gets the most engagement?
5. **Purchase path mapping**: How do they discover, evaluate, and decide to buy digital products?
