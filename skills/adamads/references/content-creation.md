# Content Creation Template Library

## 1. Short Ad Copy Templates

### Template A: Problem-Solution (PAS Framework)

**Psychology**: Validates the customer's frustration before offering relief. Creates emotional momentum toward purchase.

**Structure**: Problem → Agitate → Solution → CTA

**Template**:
```
Tired of [specific frustration]? You're not alone. [Number]% of [target audience] struggle with [problem] every single day.

That's exactly why we built [Product Name] — a [product type] that [specific benefit].

No more [pain point]. Just [desired outcome].

Get yours today at [price/link].
```

**Example**:
"Tired of spending 3 hours formatting every client proposal? You're not alone. 78% of freelancers struggle with proposal creation every single day. That's exactly why we built the Proposal Pro Toolkit — a Notion template bundle that generates polished proposals in under 15 minutes. No more staring at blank pages. Just professional results that win contracts. Get yours today at $29."

---

### Template B: Aspirational Identity

**Psychology**: Leverages identity-based motivation — people buy products that help them become who they want to be.

**Structure**: Aspirational identity → Differentiator → Feature → Transformation → Social proof → CTA

**Template**:
```
The difference between [aspirational identity] and everyone else? They don't [common struggle]. They use [Product Name].

[Key feature] helps you [specific transformation] so you can [lifestyle benefit].

Join [number] others who already made the switch.

[CTA link]
```

**Example**:
"The difference between top-earning designers and everyone else? They don't build brand guidelines from scratch every time. They use the Brand Kit Blueprint. 200+ customizable components help you deliver consistent, premium brand identities in half the time so you can take on twice the clients. Join 4,200+ designers who already made the switch. Grab yours now."

---

### Template C: Urgency-Scarcity

**Psychology**: Combines compelling offer with time/quantity pressure to accelerate decision-making. Most effective when scarcity is genuine.

**Structure**: Offer → Benefit → Price contrast → Social proof → Deadline → CTA

**Template**:
```
[Product Name] is [discount/offer] for the next [time period/quantity].

[Key benefit in one sentence].

Normally [original price], today just [sale price].

[Number] people already grabbed theirs. Don't wait — this price disappears [deadline].

[CTA link]
```

**Example**:
"The Creator Finance Dashboard is 40% off for the next 48 hours. Track income, expenses, and profit across 6 platforms in one automated sheet. Normally $49, today just $29. 1,340 creators already grabbed theirs. Don't wait — this price disappears Friday at midnight. Get it now."

## 2. Attention Hook Templates

### Hook A: Contrarian Pattern Interrupt

**Psychology**: Challenges a widely held belief. Surprise triggers curiosity and compels the viewer to keep consuming to find out why their assumption might be wrong.

**Template**: "Stop [doing what everyone recommends]. It's actually [counterintuitive truth]. Here's what works instead..."

**Examples**:
- "Stop posting every day to grow your audience. It's actually killing your engagement. Here's what works instead..."
- "Stop using free templates for your business. They're costing you more than paid ones. Here's why..."
- "Stop trying to go viral. Viral views don't pay bills. Here's what actually makes money..."

**When to use**: Best for educational content, tips, and thought leadership posts. Works on TikTok, Reels, and Twitter threads.

---

### Hook B: Specific Result Revelation

**Psychology**: Specific numbers create credibility and signal concrete, actionable information. The more specific, the more compelling.

**Template**: "How I [achieved specific result] in [timeframe] using nothing but [tool/product/method]."

**Examples**:
- "How I went from $0 to $12K/month selling Notion templates using nothing but free traffic from Pinterest."
- "How I cut my workflow from 6 hours to 45 minutes using one spreadsheet system."
- "How I got 50K views on a single post without spending a dollar on ads."

**When to use**: Best for case studies, product demos, and social proof content. Works on all platforms, especially YouTube and email.

## 3. Long-Form Product Description Template

This is the primary sales page copy for marketplace listings (Gumroad, Etsy, your website).

### Structure

```
HEADLINE: [Product Name] — [Primary Benefit in 8-12 Words]

OPENING PARAGRAPH:
If you've ever [specific frustration], you know how [emotional cost].
[Product Name] was built by [credibility statement] to solve exactly this problem.
It's a [product type] that [core transformation], so you can [aspirational outcome].

WHAT'S INSIDE:
• [Deliverable 1 with concrete numbers] — e.g., "47 pre-built templates covering 6 industries"
• [Deliverable 2 with specific result] — e.g., "Automated calculation formulas that save 3+ hours per week"
• [Deliverable 3 with tangible output] — e.g., "Step-by-step video tutorials for every component"
• [Deliverable 4...] — Continue with 5-8 items total
• Each bullet must communicate a tangible result, not just a feature

WHO THIS IS FOR:
• [Specific audience segment 1] — e.g., "Freelance designers managing 3+ client projects"
• [Specific audience segment 2] — e.g., "Agency owners who need consistent brand delivery"
• Being specific about who it's for implies who it's not for, increasing perceived value

SOCIAL PROOF:
• Used by [number]+ professionals
• Or direct testimonial quote: "[Customer result with specific numbers]" — [Customer Name, Title]

FAQ / OBJECTION HANDLING:
Q: Is this right for beginners?
A: [Address with specific onboarding/learning path included]

Q: What if I need to customize it?
A: [Highlight flexibility and ease of modification]

Q: How is this different from free templates?
A: [Name specific advantages: quality, support, updates, time saved]

Q: What if it doesn't work for me?
A: [State guarantee/refund policy]

GUARANTEE + CTA:
Try it for [number] days. If it doesn't [specific outcome], get a full refund.
Get instant access now → [Link/Button]
```

## 4. Short-Form Video Script Template

Designed for 30-60 second videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

### Structure: Hook → Value → CTA

| Seconds | Section | Purpose | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| 0-3 | Hook | Stop the scroll | "This is what my workflow used to look like..." |
| 3-8 | Transition | Introduce the product | "Then I found [Product Name]." |
| 8-25 | Demo/Value | Show it working | Screen recording of product in action |
| 25-35 | Proof | Validate the result | "Since switching, I've saved 5 hours every week." |
| 35-45 | CTA | Drive action | "Link in bio. Use code [CODE] for 20% off." |

### Video Format: Before/After Transformation

**Concept**: Show the messy "before" state, then reveal the polished "after" using your product.

**Script Template**:
```
[0-3s] "This is what my [workflow/desk/process] used to look like..." [Show chaos]
[3-5s] Quick transition effect
[5-8s] "Then I found [Product Name]."
[8-20s] [Show product in action — clean, fast, satisfying]
[20-30s] "Since switching, I've [specific result with number]."
[30-40s] "Link in bio. Use code [CODE] for [offer]."
```

### Video Format: The 3 Tips

**Concept**: Share 3 quick tips related to your product domain, with the product as the implicit solution.

**Script Template**:
```
[0-3s] "3 things I wish I knew before [activity related to product]:"
[3-12s] Tip 1: [Counterintuitive advice] — "Stop [common mistake]. Do this instead: [better approach]"
[12-22s] Tip 2: [Tool recommendation] — "Use [approach that your product enables]"
[22-32s] Tip 3: [System/framework] — "Build a [system] that handles [problem] automatically"
[32-45s] "I built [Product Name] to do all 3. Link in bio."
```

### Production Notes
- Always add captions (85% of social video is watched without sound)
- Use trending audio for algorithm boost
- End with a clear, visible link or QR code overlay
- Keep cuts fast — change visual every 2-3 seconds
- Use text overlays for key points, not just voice
